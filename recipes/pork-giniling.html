Pork Giniling

What is Giniling?

Pork Giniling (which directly translates to "ground pork meat") is a classic Filipino comfort dish, essentially a local version of picadillo. It features ground pork simmered in a savory tomato-based sauce alongside diced potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, and green peas. Often, raisins and hard-boiled eggs are added to provide delightful contrasting textures and a touch of sweetness. It is a beloved, everyday, budget-friendly meal that is hearty, satisfying, and absolutely perfect when spooned over a generous serving of steamed white rice.

Ingredients

The Meat and Base

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 medium onion (chopped)
  • 4 cloves garlic (minced)

The Sauce

  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup water or pork broth
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce (patis)

The Vegetables and Extras

  • 1 medium potato (peeled and diced into small cubes)
  • 1 medium carrot (peeled and diced into small cubes)
  • 1/2 red bell pepper (diced)
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1/4 cup raisins (optional)
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs (peeled, optional)

Seasoning

  • Salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste

Procedure

  1. Sauté the aromatics: Heat the cooking oil in a wide pan or pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and minced garlic. Sauté until the onions are soft and translucent.
  2. Brown the meat: Add the ground pork to the pan. Cook until it turns lightly brown, breaking it apart with your spatula to ensure there are no large lumps.
  3. Flavor the meat: Pour in the fish sauce and soy sauce. Stir well to coat the pork and let it cook for about 2 to 3 minutes to allow the meat to absorb the flavors.
  4. Simmer the sauce: Pour in the tomato sauce and water (or broth). Bring the mixture to a boil, then lower the heat, cover the pan, and let it simmer for 10 to 15 minutes to tenderize the pork and reduce the sauce slightly.
  5. Cook the hearty vegetables: Add the diced potatoes and carrots. Stir, cover, and continue to simmer for about 8 to 10 minutes, or until the vegetables are fork-tender.
  6. Add the remaining ingredients: Stir in the diced bell pepper, green peas, and raisins (if using). Let it cook uncovered for another 3 to 5 minutes until the peas are cooked and the sauce reaches your desired thickness.
  7. Season and serve: Taste the dish and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper as needed. Gently fold in the hard-boiled eggs to warm them through, or serve them halved on top. Serve piping hot with steamed white rice.
